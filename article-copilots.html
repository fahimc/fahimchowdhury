Copilots are a design problem before they are a tech problem

A useful copilot does not just answer faster. It understands the moment, the stakes, and the human on the other side.

FC Fahim Chowdhury · April 02, 2024
context
before clever

A copilot is not a chatbot placed beside a workflow. It is a new relationship between a person and the work they are trying to do.

That relationship needs a point of view. What should the system notice? When should it speak? What does the person need to see to stay in control?

Assistance needs timing

The strongest experiences do not respond to every possible prompt. They recognize the shape of a task and offer a useful move at the right moment.

Design the handoff, not just the answer. Let people understand why a suggestion appeared, change its direction, and dismiss it without penalty.

Trust is built into the interaction, one small choice at a time.

The model matters. But the product is the space around the model: the cues, the boundaries, and the confidence a person feels when they decide to use it.
