Stop asking where to use AI. Ask what to make possible.

Three prompts for moving your next AI conversation from a list of features to a change in behavior.

FC Fahim Chowdhury · February 19, 2024
better
questions

AI strategy often begins with a tool: a model, a platform, an API. That starting point makes it easy to build a catalogue of capabilities instead of a reason to care.

Try starting somewhere else. Ask what a person could do if they had more time, more context, or a new kind of feedback.

Three prompts to use

What decision is currently too expensive? Look for the places where time, attention, or specialist knowledge creates a bottleneck.

What would change if the first draft were free? Explore the behaviors that emerge when a person can start without fear of wasting effort.

Where should judgment remain human? Define the line that makes an intelligent system useful rather than overbearing.

Good AI strategy is a sharper question, not a longer feature list.
