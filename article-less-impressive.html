The quiet power of making AI less impressive

The most valuable AI often lowers the effort between a person and a good decision.

FC Fahim Chowdhury · May 14, 2024
less noise
more signal

We keep chasing the moment where technology takes our breath away. A machine that writes a sonnet, a model that passes an exam, a demo that makes a room go quiet.

But the AI products people return to are usually less theatrical. They remove a small uncertainty. They shorten a long search. They help someone make a decision with a little more confidence.

Useful is a feeling

When a system understands the context of a task, it does not need to announce its intelligence. It can simply put the right next step within reach.

That is the design challenge: not maximizing what a model can say, but shaping the moment so the person can do more. The best AI is often the part of the experience you stop noticing.

Make the path clearer, not the technology louder.

Start with the friction. Find the smallest intervention that changes the behavior. Then earn the right to add more.
